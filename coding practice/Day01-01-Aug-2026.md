Cloud computing basics

==================
Cloud is distributed standardised architecture ( network compute capacity ) that we do not own. 
And that we can rent depending on the need.

On-prem vs cloud?
We will choose on premise or cloud depending on requirement, budget, and privacy standards.

On premise : Local
Cloud : Connected to internet




Public Cloud: Resources are owned by a third-party cloud provider and are shared among multiple different organizations, or "tenants". The physical resources reside at the provider's location rather than within your own organization and are accessed over the public Internet.

Private Cloud: Resources are implemented and maintained internally, meaning they are dedicated entirely to a single organization. They are not shared with the public, which allows you to completely control which internal users can access specific resources, either over the Internet or an internal private network.
